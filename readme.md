welcome to accountableHer platform!

Our mission: A digital platform that monitors how institutions and the region protect women’s rights, while offering tools for reporting issues, accessing resources, and raising awareness and spreading it to make a place for women accountability everywhere

platform features:
Accountability Dashboard — Visual indicators on violence, legal protection, political participation, and economic rights.

Stories & Testimonies — A safe, anonymous space for women to share experiences.

Resource Library — Laws, international conventions, UN reports, academic studies, and guides.

Reporting Tool — A secure form for submitting cases of rights violations or institutional negligence.

Awareness Center — Articles, videos, infographics, and explainers about rights, accountability, and protection mechanisms.

Safe Voices Platform — A storytelling and support community for women.
